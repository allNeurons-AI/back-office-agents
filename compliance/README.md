# Compliance Skills

At All Neurons, we build compliance skills that handle the repetitive, high-stakes review work that compliance teams deal with every day — tracking certificates, reviewing documents, flagging obligations — without replacing the human judgment those decisions ultimately require.

| [COI Agent](./coi-agent/user-guide/readme.md) | Reads incoming Certificates of Insurance, checks them against your compliance checklist, and automatically updates your COI dashboard — flagging expired, rejected, or non-compliant certificates without manual review. |
